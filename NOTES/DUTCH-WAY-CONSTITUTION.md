# NOTES — Aparatus/The Dutch way Constitution.md (1.0, v0.1)

## What it regulates
- Owner answers: priority groups I (purpose / reliability / resilience, equal), II (economy > quality > speed), III protected (privacy, law, safety, learning, externalities).
- Rules cannot be silently overridden. "Speling" = bounded margin: within purpose, proportionate, reversible or explicitly accepted, benefit > pause cost, recordable, not consuming reserved authority. Otherwise halt pending decision.
- Override/exception record: rule, reason, calculation, accepted risk, compensating control, authority, review/expiry.
- Proportionality levels 0–3.
- Roles: Asset Owner / Asset Manager / Service Provider. "No layer may silently assume the authority of another."
- Failure transparency: failures are system data.
- Adaptive planning: fixed / planned / uncertain / reserved / review triggers.

## Objects (liftable)
- exception record, speling record, level classification, lifecycle obligation list.

## Not liftable
- "Nation" metaphor, Dutch-spirit prose, maxim tiles.
