# NOTES — G2-3 Bestuurlijke tafels, waterschappen, HWBP-alliantie

## What it regulates
- BO-MIRT: yearly agreements; only Rijk and decentral governments can table an item; spelregels are not law; bevoegd gezag takes legal decision; remedies open.
- BO Water: minister + waterschappen, provinces, municipalities, drinking water, RWS, RBOs, VRO, deltacommissaris; 3×/yr; agenda via AO/DO Water advice.
- Stuurgroep Deltaprogramma: chaired by deltacommissaris; embeds and advises.
- Waterwet 3.6a/b, 4.9, 4.10: commissioner proposes yearly, minister presents; conclusions from parliament debate become part of the programme.
- HWBP alliance: project direction at waterschap; programme directorate judges "sober en doelmatig", co-decides on deviation; escalation to programme board; CWV gives prior mandate to waterschap delegates; DGWB is system/policy responsible and principal.
- Herijking HWBP: 50-50 inlay; board sets frames, directorate executes; extra inlay needs law change; talks every 5 years (next 2029); decision on post-2036 funding by 2030.
- Waterschapswet art. 1: water-system management + waste-water treatment.

## Numbers (context only)
- ~1,400 km dike still to 2050; €14–23bn, above agreed budgets.
