# NOTES — Aparatus/The Dutch Way Corpus protocol.md (== The Dutch Way.md)

## What it regulates
- Six layers: raw, source record, extracted, claims/evidence, derived, operational use.
- Admission outcomes: admit raw, admit derived, admit restricted, quarantine, reference only, reject, do not ingest.
- Reliability tiers A–E (E = synthetic/AI). Tier E cannot establish a high-consequence fact alone.
- Sensitivity S0–S4; new S3 category needs explicit Owner decision.
- Transformation record; no derived object overwrites parents.
- Claim record with status; contradiction note; corrections append; lawful deletion leaves minimal tombstone.
- "Retrieval is not authority." "A retrieval result is not a decision."
