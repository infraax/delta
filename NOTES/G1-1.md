# NOTES — Brondossier G1-1 (Instandhouding-opdracht IenW → RWS 2024–2030)

## What it regulates
- Keep assignment: one multi-year agreement from 01-01-2024 through 2030 bundling "gewenste prestaties en bijbehorende budgetten" for exploitation, maintenance, renewal. BKN is the basis.
- Two objects: static agreement (2024–2030) vs 8-year rolling programme (2025–2033), updated yearly. Programme is part of the agreement.
- Horizon: look 16 years ahead; first 4 years "maakbaar" programmed; next 4 planned.
- Agreement binds performance + programming inside the budget position; not the budget legislator. Further growth "aan een nieuw kabinet". Uncovered work exists (portfolios, Schipholtunnel).
- ISO-55001 certification by end 2025: "niet meer haalbaar" (29 385, nr. 142). Replacement date [NF].
- Regeling agentschappen 2024 art. 6: one eindverantwoordelijke, one continuïteitsverantwoordelijke, ≥1 beleidsverantwoordelijke; art. 7: work agreements ≥ 3 calendar years.
- Continuity role approves budget, rates, plan, accounts; guards equity limits. Policy + end-responsible jointly set activities.
- "De maakbaarheid bij Rijkswaterstaat is inmiddels groter dan het financiële kader" (36 800 A, nr. 9) → "verdringing in de programmering". In 2024 the reverse was expected.
- Before 2023: 2-year horizon + "verdringingsreeks".

## Objects
- BKN (quality floor), meerjarenafspraak, meerjarenprogramma, objectbeheerregime, SAMP (internal portal), AMS, independent quality authority, UTD.

## Numbers (context only)
- Production €2.1bn (2023) → €2.6bn (2024); budget ~€3.1–3.3bn/yr 2025–2030; gap ~€34.5bn 2024–2038; 83 objects Jan 2024–Jul 2025.
